# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 26, 2026_

Accountability ("the app", "we", "us") is an accountability and habit-streak app.
This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. It is written to be
read by a human, not just a lawyer.

## What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

- **Account info** — your email address (for sign-in and verification) and your
  display name.
- **Profile photo** — if you choose to upload one.
- **Check-in proof** — the photos, optional captions, and (only when a challenge
  requires it) the location you attach to a check-in.
- **Challenge activity** — the challenges you create or join, your check-ins,
  votes/reviews, and your standing.
- **A user identifier** issued by our backend to tie the above to your account.

We do **not** collect contacts, advertising identifiers, or browsing history, and
we do **not** track you across other apps or websites.

## How we use it

Strictly to run the product: authenticate you, show your challenges and standing,
let your group review check-in proof, and send account emails (verification,
password reset). That's it.

## Money

Balances in the app are **simulated**. No real money is charged, held, or paid
out, and nothing can be withdrawn. If that ever changes, this policy and the
in-app disclosures will be updated first, and any real-money features will be
clearly separate and opt-in.

## Who can see your data

- Your display name and profile photo are visible to people in your challenges
  and to friends.
- Check-in proof photos are private to the members of that challenge.
- We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers.

We use [Supabase](https://supabase.com) as our backend and storage provider;
your data is processed on their infrastructure on our behalf.

## Storage and retention

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can **delete your account**
at any time from **Settings → Danger zone**, which removes your account and the
associated records.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your
region). Real-money features, if introduced, will require users to be 18+.

## Your choices

- Edit your display name and photo in the Profile tab.
- Decline camera, photo, or location permissions in iOS Settings (some
  check-in features will be unavailable without them).
- Delete your account in-app at any time.
