# Fair Play & Responsible Use

_Last updated: June 26, 2026_

Accountability is built to help you and your friends actually follow through on
the things you say you'll do. It is **not** a gambling app. Here's how we keep it
that way, and how you keep it healthy.

## Skill, not luck

In this app the outcome is fully in your control: you win by doing the work and
proving it, not by chance. There is no random element, no house, no odds. That is
the entire design.

## Honesty is the whole game

Challenges run on trust between people who know each other:

- **Submit real proof.** A check-in stands for something you actually did.
- **Review fairly.** When you vote on a friend's check-in, judge it honestly.
- **Only join groups you trust.** The app deliberately keeps groups small and
  private for this reason.

Faking check-ins ruins the point of the app and is a violation of the
[Terms of Service](./TERMS.md).

## About money

Right now all balances are **simulated** — practice values only. Nothing is
charged, held, or paid out. The "stake" exists to add a little pressure and
friendly rivalry, not to move real money.

If real-money features are ever added, they will come with:

- A clear opt-in, separate from simulated play.
- Age verification (18+).
- Geographic restrictions where required by law.
- Responsible-play controls and clear limits.

## Keep it healthy

Accountability should make you feel proud of showing up — not anxious or
obsessed. Set challenges you can realistically meet, support your group, and take
a break when you need one. Missing a day is not a moral failure; the streak is a
tool, not a judge.
